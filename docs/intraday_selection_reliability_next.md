## Q52 — recorded outer risk of selection procedures

### Goal and technical plan

Accept one explicit Q25, Q31 or Q36 study. Reuse the existing procedure
admission, Q35 complete three-account basis and Q12 ledger/fold reconciliation.
Describe `procedure`, `fixed_reference`, then `benchmark`, preserving their
actual policies, outer fold recipes, complete evaluated support and accounts.
Expose complete drawdown episodes and duration, daily/trade distributions,
signed cash-contribution concentration and fold diagnostics using Q12's exact
definitions. Keep descriptive account availability separate from common-basis
comparability; a failed required basis cannot be reported as a valid comparison.
An unavailable source retains its identity and reasons without inventing an
account. Do not add bootstrap intervals or subtract maximum drawdowns as a
new risk measure.

Provide a small research API and settings-independent CLI with complete JSON.
The expected scope is a dedicated procedure-risk module, a thin CLI/router,
focused regressions and this document. Native presentation belongs to Q54.

The implemented entry is `analyze_intraday_procedure_risk_diagnostics(snapshot)`;
the CLI is `market-vault research-intraday-procedure-risk-diagnostics --study PATH`.
The derived report keeps all three original `accounts`, each Q12 result under
`diagnostics`, and the original outer recipes/applications under `procedure`.
`comparability` requires the complete common basis and all three available
diagnostics. `PARTIAL` retains independently available role reports when that
requirement fails, including the case where all three accounts are individually
describable but their common price basis differs. An unavailable source gives
`UNAVAILABLE`, the original source reasons and no invented selected account.
Q25/Q31/Q36 already record summary risk metrics; Q52 adds the complete Q12
explanations rather than the first risk calculation for those procedures.

### Acceptance

1. Real Q25/Q31/Q36 reports match direct Q12 derivation on each saved account;
   independent cash/peak/duration/contribution arithmetic verifies key metrics.
2. Complete dates, folds, recipes, cash days, benchmark holding policy and
   source IDs survive; malformed basis or ledger evidence remains unavailable.
3. Source unavailability retains a meaningful report; unsupported sources fail
   admission. Instrumented boundaries prove no Q5 access, fitting, selection,
   execution, Freeze or TEST.
4. The actual CLI works without settings, returns the full deterministic report
   and fails clearly for invalid input. Existing Q12/Q35 behavior is unchanged.

## Q53 — cost sensitivity of recorded inner economic choices

### Goal and technical plan

Accept one Q31 or Q36 study; Q25's MSE objective is outside this diagnostic.
Inspect each original outer fold's inner window and the separately labelled
FINAL_DEV window. Retain the saved model(s), raw forecasts, training/holdout
support, prices and family order. Use exactly additional per-fill slippage
`0, 5, 10` bp; commission, entry/exit constraints, timing, targets, Features,
alphas and thresholds remain fixed. Q31 retains all four thresholds; Q36
retains all six models crossed with four thresholds (24 members).

At zero added cost, reexecute every member through Q6 and require complete
canonical equality with the original inner accounts, plus original selected
index and full recipe equality. Reuse the verified zero accounts. A failed
window baseline blocks that window's remaining scenarios. At nonzero costs,
reexecute every member and select maximum inner net compounded return with
FIRST_DECLARED ties. A failed member invalidates the complete window/scenario;
never remove it and select a smaller family. Retain all windows/scenarios,
unavailability and source evidence.

Report full original/new recipes, selected-index changes, member returns,
trade counts, costs, best/runner-up difference and all tied best members.
The gap is a descriptive difference of inner compounded returns, not an
out-of-sample effect or confidence interval. Count OUTER_FOLD choice changes
separately from FINAL_DEV; zero eligible windows must not appear as zero
instability. Complete derived member executions remain inspectable.

Reuse existing recorded-window, threshold transformation and Q6 primitives;
keep the orchestration in a dedicated module. Add a settings-independent CLI
and focused tests. Do not change old selection implementations or artifact
validators unless a directly necessary compatible helper extraction is proven.
No Q5, model refit, new outer account, resampling, cost optimization, automatic
Freeze/TEST or winner recommendation.

### Acceptance

1. Real Q31/Q36 complete zero-cost member accounts and recipes reproduce.
   Independent hand-computed fill costs and ranked returns verify nonzero
   scenarios and FIRST_DECLARED ties.
2. A meaningful controlled case changes an actual selected recipe with added
   cost; unchanged/tied cases remain correctly identified without assuming
   monotonic winners or profitability.
3. Complete family coverage, one-member failure, invalid policy, short-history
   unavailability and a baseline mismatch are represented without false winners.
4. No fit/Q5/source replay, no outer execution, and no TEST access occur. Saved
   inputs remain byte-identical. The real CLI returns the complete report.

### Q53 implementation and report contract

`analyze_intraday_inner_cost_sensitivity(StrategyExperiment)` and the thin
`research-intraday-inner-cost-sensitivity --study PATH` CLI accept one saved
Q31/Q36 economic procedure. The independent derived report version is
`market-vault-intraday-inner-cost-sensitivity-v1`, with its own
`sensitivity_id`; it is not a new StrategyExperiment grammar.

Every original OUTER_FOLD inner window and the separately labelled FINAL_DEV
window retains complete saved models, raw predictions, training/holdout
support and prices under `fixed_evidence`. The three declared scenarios keep
complete member executions and their original identities, full recipes,
returns, trade counts, commission and slippage in normalized cash. Q6 uses
the full saved sessions, including identity-bearing fields beyond geometry.
Zero means zero **additional** slippage: existing commission and slippage
remain in force. It verifies every complete canonical account, including all
IDs, plus the original selected index and full recipe, and reuses those
executions for the zero scenario.

A failed window baseline blocks only that window's higher-cost scenarios.
A failed pressure member retains every declared member slot and any successful
execution evidence, but leaves the entire scenario without a winner. Illegal
added policies are reported at their requested values and never clipped.
Legal whole-study unavailability retains all original windows and all three
scenario slots, including later windows whose own reason is null but whose
family was never executed; recorded member counts remain zero and no account
is invented. Source reasons and diagnostic reasons remain distinct.

`choice_changes` separates OUTER_FOLD and FINAL_DEV counts by cost. Zero
eligible windows yield null changed counts/rates and an explicit unavailable
reason. The best/runner-up gap uses the first two ranked members, including
ties, and is an inner compounded-return ratio. All exactly tied best indices
remain visible and FIRST_DECLARED chooses the original-order first member.
No gap is an outer effect, confidence interval or recommendation. FINAL_DEV
remains diagnostic only; no Q5, fitted-source replay, model fit, new outer
account, Freeze or TEST action is performed.
